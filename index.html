About JigsawFlux

JigsawFlux is a portfolio of open-source projects dedicated to solving real-world challenges in health, crisis management, and humanitarian causes.

We believe that technology should serve humanity's most pressing needs. All our projects are open-source, freely available, and built with the goal of creating positive impact rather than financial gain.

Our work spans digital health solutions, emergency response systems, crisis coordination tools, and humanitarian aid platforms. Each project is designed to be practical, accessible, and adaptable to real-world scenarios.
